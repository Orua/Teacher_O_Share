# 现有业务系统如何逐步接入 AI

## 中文

## 从一个业务动作开始

现有系统往往已经包含稳定的校验、权限和关联处理。接入 AI 时，先选一个重复发生、结果能核对的任务，例如查未完成订单、提取附件信息或修改交付日期。明确输入、预期结果和允许影响的范围，再决定需要增加什么能力。

## 通用案例：让助手处理订单变更

假设员工原来在编辑页面修改交付日期。保存时，系统还会检查订单状态、更新关联计划并记录操作。让 AI 执行同一任务前，应先追查这条完整流程。

具体做法是：

1. 让 AI 阅读页面说明、现有接口或代码，画出输入、校验、业务处理和关联影响。
2. 由业务人员确认流程，特别是不可修改状态和例外。
3. 复用已有业务服务，为助手提供范围明确的操作入口。
4. 使用相同样本，对比原操作路径与助手调用的结果。
5. 在已授权环境发布，并确认实际使用路径。

这些步骤不要求更换语言、框架、数据库或部署方式。

## AI 怎样参与

AI 可以帮助查找分散的实现、整理字段含义、编写适配和说明、准备验证样本。业务人员提供实际目标、例外规则及验收判断。涉及真实数据的修改，仍由系统依据调用者身份执行权限检查。

## 容易踩的坑

只按数据表生成接口，可能遗漏保存后的关联动作。只看到成功响应，也可能漏掉状态同步。一次修改顺带扩大成全面重构，会让原本可以验证的小目标失去边界。

另一类问题来自发布记录：工作区存在其他改动时，不能把目录当前状态全部记为已部署。发布清单应对应本次真正安装的内容。

## 可复用的经验

任务最好写清“谁在什么场景下，需要系统完成什么动作，用什么样本判断结果”。先交付一个完整的小流程，再逐步扩展。衡量进展时看业务动作是否正确完成，而不只是增加了多少接口。

本文案例为通用示例，不对应特定企业或系统，也不声称已有系统均完成了此类改造。

# Adding AI to an Existing Business System

## English

## Start with one business action

Existing systems often contain established validation, permissions, and related processing. Begin with a recurring task whose outcome can be checked: finding outstanding orders, extracting attachment information, or changing a delivery date. Define the inputs, expected result, and permitted impact before adding capabilities.

## Generic example: changing an order

Suppose an employee changes a delivery date through an editing screen. Saving also validates the order state, updates a related plan, and records the action. An assistant needs access to that complete operation.

A practical sequence is:

1. Ask AI to inspect available interface descriptions, documentation, or code and map inputs, validation, processing, and related effects.
2. Have a business owner confirm exceptions and states that prohibit changes.
3. Reuse the existing business service through a clearly scoped tool.
4. Compare the original workflow and assistant invocation using the same sample.
5. Deploy within the authorized scope and check the actual user path.

This approach does not require a particular language, framework, database, or deployment model.

## How AI participates

AI can locate scattered implementations, explain fields, prepare adapters and documentation, and propose verification samples. People supply business goals, exceptions, and acceptance judgments. The system must enforce permissions using the authenticated caller when real records are changed.

## Common pitfalls

Generating interfaces directly from tables can omit related actions performed after saving. A successful response can conceal missing state synchronization. Expanding a small task into a broad rewrite can make acceptance harder.

Release records can also become inaccurate when unrelated local changes are marked as deployed. The release list should describe what was actually installed.

## Reusable lesson

State who needs which business action, under what conditions, and which sample will demonstrate success. Deliver one complete workflow before expanding. Measure progress by correct business outcomes rather than interface counts.

This is a generic example, not a description or delivery claim about a particular organization or system.
