# Decorative & Branding Hardware

These visible parts add a brand mark, pull point or decorative detail to a product. They commonly appear on bags, cases, packaging, accessories and gifts. Start with the visible face and attachment method, then compare the shape, finish and interaction with nearby moving parts.

### Brand plate
A flat or shaped identification plate with holes, legs or an adhesive interface. Confirm the artwork and fixing method.

### Zipper pull tab
A flat pull piece with a connection hole or eye. Check its fit with the zipper puller.

### Pendant
A decorative body with a ring, chain or hook connection. Specify its hanging orientation.

### Hang tag
A metal tag intended to hang from a ring or chain. Confirm the requirements for both visible faces.

### Hanger bar
A bar-shaped fitting with end holes or mounting points for hanging or joining. Confirm its attachment layout.

### Bell charm
Bell- or dome-shaped hanging ornament. Confirm sound or movement only when documented.

### Small letter
Letter-shaped decorative part with a backing or fixing feature. Verify character artwork and alignment.

### Button
Decorative or fastening button with holes or a shank. Choose the fixing style for the host material.

### Sew-on button
Button form intended for sewing or another textile attachment. Confirm the attachment layout.

### Craft item
Standalone ornamental or gift hardware. Define the approved design and use case individually.

### Small decorative fitting
A compact decorative fitting or accessory. Confirm its structure, mounting method and appearance requirement.

## Choosing a visible part

First determine what the customer will see and how the part will be attached. A plate or tag may be sewn, riveted, glued or hung; a pull tab or charm must also fit the moving component. Approve the artwork, visible faces and edge feel before finishing.

## Easy mix-ups

A brand plate identifies the product. A hang tag or pendant hangs from a connector. A pull tab is operated by hand, while a slider travels along the zipper teeth.
