# 把业务经验整理成 AI 可执行的知识

## 中文

## 资料与执行方法之间还有一步

历史单据、表格和经验说明能提供信息，但助手还需要知道何时读取、采用哪个口径、下一步做什么，以及怎样判断完成。知识建设的目标，是让任务有明确的读取入口和执行方法。

## 通用案例：整理估价经验

某团队积累了材料、工序和外购件的估价参考。整理时，可以将知识分为四层：任务入口、核心规则、专项参考、例外与来源。

高频计算口径放进核心规则，大型价格表单独保存；入口说明在什么情况下读取参考表。客户或产品的个别样本保留适用范围，不直接上升为通用规则。获得业务负责人确认的新规则，应明确替代哪些旧假设。

另一个例子是审批助手。仅提供申请页面入口还不够，还要补齐必填资料、审批状态、可执行动作和完成检查。

## AI 怎样参与

AI 可以比较新旧材料、发现重复和矛盾、提出文件落位、更新索引，并用样例检查读取路径。业务负责人判断规则是否稳定、例外是否成立，以及新口径能否覆盖旧口径。

修改知识后，需要确认实际执行环境读取的是新版本。编辑目录中的文件与运行中的知识副本，可能不是同一份。

## 容易踩的坑

每次新增一份“最新版说明”，会形成多个平行入口。把所有大表塞进初始化上下文，会增加无关信息。把一次特殊价格写成固定规律，可能使后续估价系统性偏离。只增加新规则、不废止旧规则，会让助手随机采用不同答案。

## 可复用的经验

每条经验写明适用任务、来源、口径、操作顺序和验证方法。稳定规则、参考样本和单次任务记录分开维护。用一个新执行者测试：只读入口及指定资料，能否知道下一步怎么做、何时需要人判断、什么结果算完成。

本文使用通用估价与审批场景，不含任何企业的价格、客户或流程细节。

# Turning Business Experience into Actionable AI Knowledge

## English

## Information needs an execution method

Historical records, spreadsheets, and notes provide information. An assistant also needs to know when to read them, which definitions apply, what to do next, and how to recognize completion. A useful knowledge base provides both an entry point and a method.

## Generic example: organizing estimation knowledge

A team has reference material for materials, processing, and purchased components. Organize it into task entry points, core rules, specialized references, and exceptions with provenance.

Keep frequent calculation rules in the core material and store large reference tables separately. Explain when to load each table. Preserve the limits of individual customer or product examples instead of turning them into universal rules. When a business owner confirms a new rule, specify which assumptions it replaces.

An approval assistant needs more than a link to a form. It also needs required information, approval states, permitted actions, and completion checks.

## How AI participates

AI can compare materials, identify duplication and contradictions, suggest where updates belong, maintain indexes, and check retrieval with sample tasks. A business owner decides whether a rule is stable, whether an exception is valid, and whether a new definition supersedes an old one.

After updating knowledge, confirm that the execution environment reads the new version. An edited file and a running knowledge copy may differ.

## Common pitfalls

Repeatedly creating another “latest guide” produces competing entry points. Loading every reference table at initialization adds irrelevant context. Treating a special price as a general rule can bias later estimates. Adding new instructions without retiring old ones creates inconsistent answers.

## Reusable lesson

Record the task, source, definitions, procedure, and verification method for each lesson. Maintain stable rules, reference examples, and one-time task records separately. Ask a new executor to use only the entry point and designated material: can it identify the next action, recognize when human judgment is needed, and check completion?

These estimation and approval examples contain no organization-specific prices, customers, or procedures.
