# 如何组织 AI 完成跨模块开发

## 中文

## 拆分任务之后，还需要集成责任

跨模块项目常同时包含文档、权限、界面、业务处理、性能和发布。拆开工作有助于并行，但每项都需要明确输入、输出、依赖和接收人。

## 通用案例：一次多模块升级

假设升级分为使用说明、公开入口、功能基线、计算修复、性能优化和最终集成。计算修复依赖可靠基线；公开入口依赖访问规则；最终集成依赖各模块可用产物。

先画依赖表。互不影响的调查和修改可以并行，有前置条件的任务先做准备。采用何种版本管理或工作区隔离工具可以自行选择，关键是避免多个执行者在同一处无协调修改。

## AI 怎样参与

AI 可以分头调查、实现独立模块、整理差异和运行定向检查。协调者持续检查依赖，确认哪些成果可以集成，并验证用户实际路径。业务负责人决定优先级、范围和验收条件。

如果改为集中接管，应停止原执行者，记录最后状态，再交接继续，避免旁路任务仍在修改。

## 容易踩的坑

第一，测试工具本身成为新项目，消耗大量时间。应先确认现有检查能支持哪些结论，再决定是否补工具。

第二，验收器有错却被当作产品裁判。先用已知正确和已知错误样本检查验收器，才能依赖其结果。

第三，一条评测链路阻塞，其他无依赖工作也一起停下。阻塞应绑定具体依赖。

第四，构建成功、成果收拢或局部性能改善，被概括为整体完成。局部收益需要保留适用场景，完整验收缺口仍应列出。

## 可复用的经验

每项任务写明负责范围、输入版本、产物、完成条件和集成方式。状态至少区分已实现、已验证、待集成和具体阻塞。

并发量以审阅与集成能力为限。定期检查可用产物及未完成事项，而不是只看活动对话数量。多人或多 Agent 协作都需要一个对最终结果负责的接收角色。

本文为通用项目组织示例，未使用真实仓库、分支、工时或性能数据。

# Organizing AI Work Across Modules and Repositories

## English

## Task division still needs integration ownership

Cross-module projects may involve documentation, permissions, user interfaces, business processing, performance, and release work. Parallel execution helps only when each task has defined inputs, outputs, dependencies, and an owner who accepts the result.

## Generic example: a multi-module upgrade

Imagine separate tasks for instructions, public access, a functional baseline, computation fixes, performance, and final integration. Fixes depend on a reliable baseline; public access depends on access rules; integration depends on usable outputs from the other tasks.

Map dependencies first. Independent investigations and changes can run in parallel, while dependent tasks prepare their inputs. Choose appropriate workspace isolation and version-management tools; the essential requirement is to prevent uncoordinated edits.

## How AI participates

AI can investigate, implement independent modules, explain differences, and run targeted checks. A coordinator verifies dependencies, accepts integration-ready outputs, and checks the actual user path. Business owners set priorities, scope, and acceptance conditions.

When work is centralized again, stop previous executors, record their last state, and hand over before continuing.

## Common pitfalls

Building evaluation infrastructure can consume the project. Establish what existing checks can prove before expanding them.

An evaluator can also be wrong. Validate it with known-good and known-bad samples before treating its output as authoritative.

A blocked evaluation path should not halt unrelated work. Tie blockers to specific dependencies.

Finally, successful builds, collected changes, or local performance gains do not establish completion of the full upgrade. Preserve the scope of measured benefits and list remaining acceptance gaps.

## Reusable lesson

Define ownership, input versions, deliverables, acceptance, and integration for each task. Distinguish implemented, verified, ready for integration, and specifically blocked work.

Limit concurrency to review and integration capacity. Inspect usable outputs and outstanding requirements rather than counting active conversations. Human teams and AI teams both need ownership of the final result.

This generic scenario contains no real repositories, branches, work durations, or performance data.
