# 让 AI 修改业务数据：身份、执行与回读

## 中文

## 写入需要可核对的全过程

助手能够调用修改工具，只说明入口可用。正式执行还需要核对身份、目标记录、业务条件、影响和结果。通用流程是：读取当前状态，确认修改范围，执行，回读，记录结果。

## 通用案例：批量更新业务记录

假设需要修正一批记录中的分类。执行前先读取目标 ID 和当前分类，形成变更清单；业务人员确认映射与例外；系统依据调用者权限执行；每批返回受影响记录；回读检查新值，并记录进度。

批次大小取决于接口能力、数据关系和恢复方式。中断后先检查上一批是否已生效，再决定是否重试。支持幂等标识或可靠去重时，可以进一步降低重复操作风险；不能仅凭“已经分批”就假定具备幂等性。

## AI 怎样参与

AI 帮助整理字段映射、发现缺失值、生成待执行清单、调用工具并汇总异常。人确认业务含义、歧义对象及真实影响。服务端负责权限、状态限制和数据一致性。

操作者身份来自可信认证上下文。业务参数中的负责人、目标用户或组织编号，不应覆盖实际操作者身份。

## 容易踩的坑

接口实现和调用验证通过后，仍可能遗漏原有审计页面需要的记录。新增、修改、删除的成功与失败，都应按照既定审计要求记录动作、对象、影响数量和结果，避免日志只覆盖部分操作。

超时也不等于写入失败。没有明确回执时立即重发，可能产生重复记录。只看返回状态而不回读业务结果，则可能漏掉部分成功或关联更新失败。

## 可复用的经验

写入任务说明至少包含目标对象、字段、执行前状态、允许范围、回读方式和中断恢复方法。测试资料要有识别方式，并明确是否会进入正式统计以及如何清理或恢复。

这里介绍的是通用控制思路。具体权限、保留周期、批次大小和恢复方案，应由使用系统的业务与数据要求确定。

# AI Data Changes: Identity, Execution, and Readback

## English

## Make the whole change observable

An assistant's ability to call an update tool only proves that an entry point exists. Production execution also requires identity, target records, business conditions, impact, and results to be checked. A general sequence is: read the current state, confirm the scope, execute, read back, and record the result.

## Generic example: updating records in batches

Suppose a group of records needs a corrected classification. Read the target IDs and current values, prepare a change list, and have the mapping and exceptions reviewed. The system applies caller permissions, returns affected records for each batch, and supports readback and progress tracking.

Batch size depends on interface capacity, data relationships, and recovery needs. After an interruption, determine whether the previous batch took effect before retrying. Idempotency identifiers or reliable deduplication can reduce duplicate changes; batching alone does not provide that guarantee.

## How AI participates

AI can prepare field mappings, identify missing values, produce the proposed change list, invoke tools, and summarize exceptions. People resolve business ambiguity and confirm impact. The service enforces permissions, state restrictions, and consistency.

The acting identity must come from trusted authentication context. Owner, target-user, or organization parameters must not replace the authenticated caller.

## Common pitfalls

A working interface may still omit records expected by the existing audit workflow. Creation, update, and deletion outcomes should follow the applicable audit requirements, including the action, target, affected count, and result.

A timeout does not prove that a write failed. Retrying without a receipt can duplicate changes. Checking only response status can miss partial success or failed related updates.

## Reusable lesson

Specify target records, fields, previous state, permitted scope, readback, and interruption recovery. Make test data recognizable, explain whether it affects production reporting, and define cleanup or restoration.

These are general controls. Permissions, retention, batch size, and recovery must fit the business and data requirements of the system in use.
