# Locks & Closures

These parts allow a user to close, secure or release a bag, case, pouch, box or cover. Start with the action—press, twist, slide, pull, use a magnet or turn a key—then compare the two mating parts and the available mounting space.

### Insert lock
Male and female parts that engage through insertion. Verify the engagement depth and release action.

### Twist lock
A closure with a rotating element. Confirm its travel, stop position and clearance.

### Press lock
A closure that latches by pressing or snapping. Test the opening force and repeated operation.

### Magnetic snap
Two mating parts held together by magnets. Check the alignment, gap and retention in the main product.

### Drawstring stopper
A part with cord passages and a spring-operated release. Match the hole size to the cord.

### Snap fastener
A male-and-female press-stud closure. Select it according to the material thickness and setting method.

### General lock
A general label for a closure that has not yet been assigned a more specific action. Confirm it with a drawing or sample.

### Hinge
Leaves joined by a pin to permit rotation. Define opening angle, clearance and mounting holes.

## Choosing a closure

Start with the user action: press, twist, slide, pull a cord, use a magnet or insert a key. Check the two mating parts and the space available on the product. These forms commonly appear on bags, cases, pouches, boxes and hinged covers.

## Easy mix-ups

A magnetic snap holds two faces together but is not the same as a mechanical lock. A hinge controls movement but does not necessarily close it.
